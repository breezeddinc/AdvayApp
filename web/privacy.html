This Privacy Policy explains what ADVAY does — and doesn't — do with your information. ADVAY is a client for the Bluesky network, built on the AT Protocol.

1. Data held by Bluesky, not ADVAY

When you use ADVAY, your account, posts, follows, likes, and direct messages are created and stored directly on Bluesky's servers (your Personal Data Server), not on servers operated by ADVAY. That data is governed by Bluesky's own Privacy Policy. ADVAY never sees your password — login happens directly against Bluesky's servers.

  • Crash and performance data: if enabled, used only to fix bugs — not shared or sold.

ADVAY does not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies and local storage

The web version of ADVAY uses local storage in your browser to keep you signed in and remember your preferences.

4. Your choices

You can delete your Bluesky account and its data at any time through Bluesky's own account settings, independent of ADVAY. You can also simply stop using ADVAY and use another Bluesky client instead — your account and content move with you.

5. Children's privacy

ADVAY is not directed at children under 13, consistent with Bluesky's own minimum age requirements.
